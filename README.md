## 👨‍⚕️ About Me
I am an academic clinician-scientist leveraging machine learning, information technology, and large-scale clinical trials to optimize perioperative care, enhance clinical workflows, and improve patient safety. 

My work spans the deployment of real-time predictive analytics within the Electronic Health Record (EHR), the rigorous evaluation of perioperative telemedicine systems, and specialized clinical care in high-acuity abdominal transplant and vascular anesthesia.

---

## 🔬 Core Research Programs

### 1. Perioperative Telemedicine & Pragmatic Clinical Trials
* **Large-Scale Evaluation:** Served as first author on two pivotal, large-scale randomized controlled trials (**ACTFAST-3** and **TECTONICS**) evaluating intraoperative telemedicine support encompassing **98,000 patients**.
* **EHR Data Engineering:** Developed the real-time informatics data pipelines and final statistical analysis plans to evaluate perioperative quality measures and patient outcomes.
* **Evidence-Based De-implementation:** Rigorously demonstrated a lack of impact on patient outcomes, leading to the data-driven de-implementation of the operational unit, saving substantial institutional clinical effort.

### 2. Machine Learning Development in Perioperative Care
* **Architectural Innovation:** Collaborated with computer science teams to build novel neural network architectures handling high-frequency physiologic data and multi-modal tabular/textual integration (e.g., contrastive learning, graph neural networks).
* **Robustness & Fairness:** Spearheaded methods to resolve real-time clinical deployment issues, including missing data mitigation, tracking semantic database shifts during Epic transitions, and evaluating algorithmic bias/fairness.
* **Data Integration:** Built an integrated repository of over 180,000 patient records to train and validate production-ready predictive models for postoperative complications.

### 3. Workflow Automation & Postoperative Handoffs (The ECHO Project)
* **Adaptive Tools:** Investigated the integration of AI-driven risk stratification into critical care transitions to replace rigid, burden-heavy checklists.
* **Human-Centered AI:** Used qualitative methods to co-design a flexible "AI risk report card" highlighting adverse outcome probabilities for bedside nursing handoffs.
* **Active Trials:** Serve as Co-Investigator on the AHRQ-funded **ECHO project (1R01HS029324-01)**, implementing the data and ML inference pipelines for OR-to-ICU handoffs.

### 4. Machine Learning in Peripartum Care
* **Automating Data Extraction:** Created a custom screen-automation and computer-vision data-scraping engine to bypass vendor constraints, extracting electronic fetal monitoring (EFM) waveforms for a 5,000-patient query.
* **Fetal Risk Stratification:** Leading a project supported by WUSTL TRIADS using EFM data and ML modeling to improve the accuracy and interpretability of identifying fetal distress, predicting unplanned cesarean sections, and low neonatal cord blood pH.

### 5. Clinical Epidemiology & Biostatistical Leadership
* **Core Leadership:** Core Quantitative Methods Leader for the Center for Perioperative Mental Health (P50) renewal, designing statistical plans and power analyses for multiple R34 projects and an R01 signature project.
* **Departmental Governance:** Serve as the Chair of Regulatory Scientific Review for the Division of Clinical and Translational Research, managing pre-IRB scientific review pipelines to ensure methodological rigor.

## 🏥 Clinical & Educational Leadership
* **Sub-specialty Focus:** Maintain an active clinical practice (comprising the majority of my time) in the General Anesthesiology Division at **Barnes-Jewish Hospital** (Pod Leader for the 19-OR area spanning Spine, Neurosurgery, ENT, Hepatobiliary, and Transplant) and **Progress West Hospital**.
* **Transplant Care:** Provide specialized anesthesia care for complex abdominal transplant procedures, personally delivering care for a significant portion of all liver transplants at BJH (21% of the total in 2025). 
* **Protocol Innovation:** Developed and maintain the comprehensive liver transplant resident protocol, modified protocols for the world's first reported total robotic liver transplant, and structured donor/recipient pathways for adult living donors.
* **Trainee Mentorship:** Regularly supervise residents and SRNAs on 67% of clinical days, moderate monthly morbidity and mortality conferences, and have guided multiple graduate and post-doctoral scholars successfully into positions across academia and industry.
